## Diagnosis (reproduced)

**Trigger:** `watchdog_stall` — a single skill (`catch_Psyduck`, name scrubbed to `stall_a`) made zero observable progress for 30,000 frames inside the **Lavender Town Pokémon Center** (map (8,0)).

**Root cause (verified by headless reproduction from `stall_a.ss1`):** `dexbot/planner.py::missing_catchable()` emits **surf-method encounters without any Surf-capability gate**. Rod methods are skipped when the rod isn't owned (planner.py:123); surf has no equivalent check — only *land* reachability. So **Psyduck on Route 22 (surf-only, 100% slot rate)** sorts to the **queue head**, but the bot can't surf (no HM03, `BADGE05_GET=False`). `catch_Psyduck` then freezes 30k frames (its surf path lacks the fast-fail guard the fishing path has, so it loops `navigate_to` over unreachable water tiles instead of deferring cleanly). This blocks the run: the reproduction showed that the moment Psyduck is removed from the queue, **`catch_Gastly` and `catch_Doduo` immediately succeed** — 6 of the 8 queue entries are genuinely catchable now (Silph Scope is owned, unlocking the tower).

This is the **first live manifestation** of the exact defect the two BASELINE test failures already encode.
